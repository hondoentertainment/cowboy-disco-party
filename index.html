Wardrobe

The Dress Code

Western glamour meets Studio 54 — dress for the lights.

Dress country. Shine disco.

Think elevated western glamour — champagne gold, ivory, saddle leather, and mirror-ball chrome. Cowboy hats, fringe, and rhinestones are all on-theme. If it catches the light, you're dressed right.

  • Champagne gold and warm metallics — hats, belts, jewelry
  • White cowboy boots and crisp ivory tops for contrast
  • Chrome sequins, rhinestones, and subtle cow-print accents
  • Black denim, leather, and bold western silhouettes welcome

When in doubt, add one more layer of sparkle.

Six Looks

Six ways to land the theme — from denim disco to outlaw elegance. Wear one head to toe, mix two, or treat them as a starting point. Every piece is a suggestion, not a rule.

FAQ

Quick answers before you hit the dance floor.

Where do I go?

Head to 420 NE 72nd St, Seattle — look for the Cowboy Disco signs and follow the music to the dance floor.

Where are the bathrooms?

Just ask a host — they'll point you to the nearest one.

How do I vote for best outfit?

Hosts hand out numbered tags at the door. Enter that contestant number on the Best Outfit Vote page before voting closes.

Are there prizes for best outfit?

Yes — 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place prizes for best outfit. Vote by contestant number before voting closes at 9 PM. Bring your A-game fringe and sequins.

Can Kyle and Jeremy help plan my next party?

They might! Kyle and Jeremy can help plan your next party — if you ask them nice.

What about food allergies?

Just ask! Any of the cooks are happy to tell you what's in the food so you can eat safely.
