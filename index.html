Wardrobe

The Dress Code

Western glamour meets Studio 54 — dress for the lights.

Dress country. Shine disco.

Think elevated western glamour — champagne gold, ivory, saddle leather, and mirror-ball chrome. Cowboy hats, fringe, and rhinestones are all on-theme. If it catches the light, you're dressed right.

  • Champagne gold and warm metallics — hats, belts, jewelry
  • White cowboy boots and crisp ivory tops for contrast
  • Chrome sequins, rhinestones, and subtle cow-print accents
  • Black denim, leather, and bold western silhouettes welcome

When in doubt, add one more layer of sparkle.

FAQ

Quick answers before you hit the dance floor.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 6:00 PM. Formal party introduction and ice breakers are at 7:15 PM, and 8:30 PM is the outfit contest and happy birthdays (live applause). Last dance is 10:00 PM.

Where do I go?

Head to Green Lake Village East, 427 NE 72nd St, Seattle. Use intercom pin 340397, then look for the Cowboy Disco signs and follow the music to the dance floor. Map and directions are on this page.

What should I wear?

Dress country, shine disco — cowboy hats, fringe, rhinestones, and anything that catches the light. See the dress code for outfit ideas. There are prizes for best outfit.

Do I need an app?

No app. Open this site on your phone for the photo booth, photo gallery, and ice breakers. You can add it to your home screen if you like.

What's the intercom pin?

Enter 340397 on the intercom at Green Lake Village East.

Is there parking?

Street parking is available around the venue. Please be a good neighbor and watch for signed restrictions.

Where are the bathrooms?

Just ask a host — they'll point you to the nearest one.

How does the outfit contest work?

Live applause at 8:30 PM, then happy birthdays. Contestants take the floor, the room cheers, and the loudest look wins. No phone vote and no contestant numbers.

Are there prizes for best outfit?

Yes — 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place prizes for best outfit, judged by live applause at 8:30, right before happy birthdays. Bring your A-game fringe and sequins.

Can Kyle and Jeremy help plan my next party?

They might! Kyle and Jeremy can help plan your next party — if you ask them nice.

What about food allergies?

Just ask! Any of the cooks are happy to tell you what's in the food so you can eat safely.

When is the next party?

We're still deciding! Visit our poll to vote on when it should be held and give us feedback on how this one went.
